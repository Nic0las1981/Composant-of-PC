Meshify 2

The new Meshify 2 is a classic evolved with focus on distinctive design, spacious interior and rugged good looks.

Type

A classic evolved

Detachable front filter

The new front nylon filter can easily be removed from the hinged mesh panel for increased airflow.

Superlative storage

Convert to Storage Layout and install up to 11 HDDs alongside four dedicated SSDs mounts. (6x SSD/HDD trays, 2x SSD brackets and 1x Multibracket included)

Extremely flexible

Transformable interior design utilizing a movable wall for enhancing custom builds.

Type-C interface

Front I/O offers easy access to ports, including one USB Type-C with speeds up to 20 Gbps.

Smooth airflow

Three Dynamic X2 GP-14 fans come preinstalled with room to expand up to a total of nine fans.

Versatile multibracket

The included Multibracket lets you convert any unused fan position to an HDD, SSD or pump mount.
