Terra

Inspired by the homes of modern gamers, Terra is a small form factor case crafted to lift the desk environment. It incorporates boutique design features such as thick panels cut from anodized aluminum and FSC-certified solid walnut. The easily accessible interior features a slidable central wall and space for a powerful GPU.

Type

Crafted to elevate

Elegantly crafted

Featuring panels cut from FSC-certified solid walnut and anodized aluminum, including an 8 mm-thick aluminum front.

Engineered for SFF gaming

Enjoy powerful, uncluttered gaming, with the ability to install a GPU up to 322 mm long in a space-saving 10.4 L case.

Easily accessible

Slide open the top with an integrated tab and lift open the side panels for easy access to your components.

Choose your accent

Adjustable motherboard plate

Front-facing I/O

Featuring an aluminum power button and two USB ports for connecting devices, including one USB Type-C 20Gbps.

Transportable

Features the ability to lock the top and side panels for safer transportation of your build.
