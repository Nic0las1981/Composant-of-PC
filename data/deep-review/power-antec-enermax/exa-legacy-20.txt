ZDPMS (Zero Delay Power Monitor System)

ENERMAX DIGIFANLESS is the world’s first digital monitor controlled fanless power supply. The proprietary ZDPMS (Zero Delay Power Monitoring System) software allows user to read the real-time PSU power consumption, efficiency and internal temperature. The DC output current, voltage protection warning points and 12V rail(s) can also be manually configured.
