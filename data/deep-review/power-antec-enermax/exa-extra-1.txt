 The Antec NE1000G M ATX 3.0 is a high-performance, fully modular power supply unit (PSU) designed to meet the demands of gamers and PC enthusiasts. 

- ### ATX 3.0 & PCIe 5.0 Compatibility

 Meets ATX 3.0 standards for reliability and power efficiency; includes a PCIe 5.0 12VHPWR GPU cable for seamless compatibility with modern graphics cards like the NVIDIA® GeForce RTX™ 40 Series. 
- ### 80 PLUS® Gold Certification

 Delivers up to 92% efficiency, reducing electricity bills and minimizing heat generation. 
- ### High-Quality Japanese Capacitors

 Ensures tight voltage regulation and minimal ripple and noise, maximizing system performance. 
- ### PhaseWave™ Design

 Incorporates a server-class LLC design with synchronous rectification based on a DC-DC topology, enhancing performance and stability. 
- ### Zero RPM Manager

 Advanced thermal control enables the fan to remain off during low loads, ensuring silent operation and extended lifespan. 
- ### 120mm Fluid Dynamic Bearing (FDB) Silent Fan

 Provides high airflow with durability and longevity while maintaining quiet operation. 
- ### CircuitShield™ Protection

 Offers industrial-grade protections, including Over Current Protection (OCP), Over Voltage Protection (OVP), Under Voltage Protection (UVP), Short Circuit Protection (SCP), Over Power Protection (OPP), and Over Temperature Protection (OTP), safeguarding your system in various environments. 
- ### Fully Modular Design

 Allows users to connect only the necessary cables, reducing clutter and improving airflow within the PC case. 
- ### 10-Year Warranty

 Backed by Antec's 10-year warranty, reflecting the company's commitment to quality and customer satisfaction.
