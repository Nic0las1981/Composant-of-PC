## HCP-750

##### HIGH CURRENT PRO SERIES

Continuous Power: Trust Antec to label all of our power supply units in Continuous Power, the steady power a PSU can output continuously, rather than the amount delivered at its short-lived peak.

Intelligent Hybrid Cable Management: The HCP series features a heavy-gauge 16 AWG High Current cable for high performance components to reduce conducted resistance. A modular 10-pin connector system allows you to directly connect either 10-pin or 5-pin connector harnesses.

NVIDI® SLI® -Ready Certification: Only PSUs that successfully pass rigorous testing can be NVIDIA® SLI® -Ready certified, ensuring a quality user experience.
