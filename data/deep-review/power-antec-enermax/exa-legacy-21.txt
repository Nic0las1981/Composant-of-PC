The DIGIFANLESS 550 Watt is the first digital and fanless power supply from Enermax. Its powerful passive cooling system allows completely silent operation with 0 dB(A). State-of-the-art technologies and long-lasting quality components ensure an extremely stable and energy-saving operation with up to 93% efficiency (80PLUS® Platinum). The power supply has a digital interface for reading and controlling central power values (e.g. voltage, efficiency or temperature) via the new ZDPMS software from Enermax. The fully modular cable management with the individually sleeved cables offers the greatest possible comfort during the system build.

## Efficiency 

The 80PLUS® Platinum-certified power supply achieves up to 93% efficiency at 230VAC between 20 and 100% load. This not only results in low heat generation but also reduces its own CO2 footprint.

DIGIFANLESS helps systems to meet the EU eco-design directive 2013 ErP Lot 6 and 2014 ErP Lot 3 (< 0.5W in standby mode*) due to a high-efficient 5V standby (+5Vsb) circuitry. Moreover, it supports EU regulation no. 617/2013 demanding amongst others 85% efficiency at 50% load and 82% at 20% and 100% load.

* only in combination with an ErP ready mainboard

 

## Power 

DIGIFANLESS supplies secure and stable power thanks to two massive 12V rails with separate over-current protection (OCP). Optional the rails can be switch to single-rail operation via the ZDPMS software to run PC systems with high performance or overclocked graphics cards and CPUs.

The DC-to-DC design generates the secondary voltages (+5V and +3.3V) from the primary voltage rail (+12V). The positive effect is higher efficiency, clean output voltages (low ripple noise) and perfect voltage regulation at all loads.

The Copper Bridge Array is an innovative and patented transmission technology for direct voltage transmission to reduce the wire-resistance and to increase the efficiency. Optimized air circulation due to clear and spacious PCB design.

 

## Connectivity 

The black cables are individually sleeved, which gives them a high-end look. In addition, they are flatter and more flexible than conventionally sleeved cables and can therefore be laid better behind the cladding.

The fully modular cable management allows an easy system installation and cable routing and improves the air circulation inside the case.

 

## Coooling 

The sophisticated passive cooling system of the DIGIFANLESS power supply guarantees a completely silent operation (0db(A)). The PSU is, therefore, the first choice for all silent PCs, multimedia stations or HTPCs.

 

## Digital Interface 

DIGIFANLESS is equipped with a digital interface to transmit and control the most essential performance values. With the ZDPMS software (“Zero-Delay Power Monitoring System”) users can easily monitor the power supply operation and customize certain functions, eg. switch between multi- or single-rail mode, fine-tune the 12V output voltage or set the trigger points for OCP/OVP warning messages. More information about the various function of the ZDPMS software you can find in the ZDPMS manual.

 

## Compatibility 

Intel® ATX12V v2.4, compliant with the Intel Power Supply Design Guide for Desktop Platform Form Factors Revision 1.31 April 2013.

Full support of the C6/C7 energy-saving functions of Intel® processors, first introduced with the Haswell™ generation.

Worldwide compatibility with automatic 100-240VAC input and up to 0.99 with active Power Factor Correction (PFC).

 

## Quality & Safety 

5 Years Manufacturer Warranty.

Enermax only uses high-quality components including first-class Japanese 105°C electrolytic capacitors for maximum stability and durability.

Enermax SafeGuard, multiple protection circuit that keeps the power supply and the system safe from damage by short circuit (SCP), over voltage (OVP), under voltage (UVP), over current (OCP), over temperature (OTP), over power (OPP) as well as surges and inrush current (SIP).

Non-Stop 24/7 industrial class performance at 40°C ambient temperature.
