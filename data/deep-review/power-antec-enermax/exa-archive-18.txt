# Enermax - REVOBRON TGA EDITION
URL: https://www.enermaxeu.com/products/power-supplies/mainstream/revobrontga/index.html

Enermax - REVOBRON TGA EDITION

 

 

 

 

 

REVOBRON TGA is the TUF Gaming Alliance Edition of the popular REVOBRON Series. TGA is a cooperation with ASUS to provide specially chosen hardware for your gaming PC. TGA hardware has been tested and fine-tuned to fit the design and the tough demands for gaming machines.

REVOBRON TGA is equipped with durable quality components and sophisticated technical design to ensure stable and clean output voltages. The REVOBRON TGA series has the international energy-saving certificate 80 PLUS® Bronze and comes with two independent +12V rails to safely empower systems with high power consumption in 24/7 operation. The semi-modular cable management with flexible and flat cables offers the best options for system installation or expansion.

REVOBRON TGA comes with special accessories: COOLERGENIE: The intelligent 2-in-1 fan controller that enables semi-fanless and fan-delay function for external fans. T.B.RGB: 3x 12cm fans with RGB LEDs ready for synchronization with RGB motherboards from ASUS, ASRock, MSI and Gigabyte.

Efficiency

The 80PLUS® Bronze-certified power supply achieves 84 to 91% efficiency at 230VAC between 20 and 100% load. This not only results in low heat generation but also reduces its own CO2 footprint.

REVOBRON TGA helps systems to meet the EU eco-design directive 2013 ErP Lot 6 and 2014 ErP Lot 3 (< 0.5W in standby mode*) due to a high-efficient 5V standby (+5Vsb) circuitry. Moreover, it supports EU regulation no. 617/2013 demanding amongst others 85% efficiency at 50% load and 82% at 20% and 100% load.

* only in combination with an ErP ready mainboard

 

Power

The DC-to-DC design generates the secondary voltages (+5V and +3.3V) from the primary voltage rail (+12V). The positive effect is higher efficiency, clean output voltages (low ripple noise) and perfect voltage regulation at all loads.

99 % of the PSU power can be delivered by a multi-rail design with two stable 12V rails to safely empower high-performance processors and graphics cards.

 

Connectivity

Modular & Flat Cables simplify the cable routing and improve the airflow inside the case.

Extended 70cm CPU connector cable for installation in large cases.

 

Cooling

The integrated 12cm Enermax fan with patented Twister Bearing™ technology ensures an efficient and continuously silent cooling and long lifetime with up to 160,000 hours MTBF.

Silent and powerful cooling performance due to the advanced and intelligent SpeedGurad fan control.

 

Coolergenie

Coolergenie is a semi-fanless fan controller for up to three external fans. Monitoring the CPU fan speed via PWM, the case fans start to spin after reaching 40% (Whisper Mode) or 55% (Silence Mode) of CPU fan speed. Coolergenie also provides a fan delay function to cool the system 40 to 60 seconds after shut down. This can prolong the lifetime of your components.

 

T.B.RGB 3 Fan Pack

The included T.B.RGB 3 Fan Pack offers full colour control and stunning visual effects. Four halo shaped rings equipped with the ENERMAX LED Lighting Technology are smoothly worked into the frame and offer bright and even illumination without visible LED spots.

The RGB synchronization via 4-pin RGB header is compatible with motherboards using Asus Aura Sync, ASRock RGB LED, MSI Mystic Light Sync and Gigabyte RGB Fusion.

Note: T.B.RGB 3 Fan Pack doesn’t include an RGB controller. A separated controller or a compatible motherboard with RGB header is needed for the RGB lighting.

 

Compatibility

Intel® ATX12V v2.4, compliant with the Intel Power Supply Design Guide for Desktop Platform Form Factors Revision 1.31 April 2013.

Full support of the C6/C7 energy-saving functions of Intel® processors, first introduced with the Haswell™ generation.

Worldwide compatibility with automatic 100-240VAC input and up to 0.99 with active Power Factor Correction (PFC).

 

Quality & Safety

3 Years Manufacturer Warranty.

Enermax only uses high-quality components including first-class Japanese 105°C electrolytic capacitors for maximum stability and durability.

Enermax SafeGuard, the multiple protection circuit that keeps the power supply and the system safe from damage by short circuit (SCP), over voltage (OVP), under voltage (UVP), over current (OCP), over temperature (OTP), over power (OPP) as well as surges and inrush current (SIP).

Non-Stop 24/7 industrial class performance. Full rated power output at 40°C ambient temperature.
