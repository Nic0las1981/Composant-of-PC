### Product Introduction

The Revolution X’t II power supply series from ENERMAX achieves a peak efficiency of 92% (80 PLUS® Gold certified). It is based on an advanced DC-to-DC circuit for perfect voltage regulation and full compatibility with latest CPU/GPU energy-saving modes. The integrated 13.9cm Twister bearing fan ensures an efficient cooling and a silent operation. Revolution X’t II is built for performance-capable gaming system with one or two graphics cards. The flat modular cables simplify the system installation and cable routing. ENERMAX grants 5 years warranty on the Revolution X’t II series.

### Efficiency

- 80 PLUS® Gold Up to 92 percent efficiency at 230V and 20 to 100 percent load. 80 PLUS® Gold certified.
- Support of EU Eco-Design Regulations The Revolution X't II Series helps systems to meet the EU eco-design directive ErP Lot 6 2013 (< 0.5W in standby mode*) due to a high-efficient 5V standby (+5Vsb) circuitry as well as the EU regulation no. 617/2013 demanding amongst others 85% efficiency at 50% load and 82% at 20% and 100% load. * only in combination with an ErP Lot 6 2013 ready mainboard 

### Stability

- DC-to-DC Design The secondary voltages (+5V and +3.3V) are generated by the primary voltage rail (+12V). Positive effect: Higher efficiency, clean output voltages (low ripple & noise) and a perfect voltage regulation at all loads.
- Full GPU & CPU Power Massive 12V rail to power high-performance and overclocked processors and graphics cards. More than 98 percent of the total power of the PSU can be delivered by the 12V rail only. 

### Cooling

- Air Cooling by Enermax The integrated 13.9cm fan with patented Twister Bearing Technology ensures an efficient and continuously silent cooling and long lifetime.
- SpeedGuard Ultra silent and powerful cooling performance due to an advanced and intelligent fan speed control with maximum 1000 RPM (450/550W) or 1500 RPM (650/750W) and minimum 450 RPM (450/550W) and minimum 600 RPM (650/750W).
- HeatGuard (patented) Keeping PSU fan running for 30-60 seconds after shut-down to dissipate the remaining system heat and prolonging the system's lifetime. 

### Connectivity

- Semi-Modular Cables Management with Flat Cables Simplify the cable routing and enhance the airflow inside the case.
- Multi-GPU Support Min. two 6+2P (8P) PCI-E connectors to power the latest high-end graphics cards and multi-GPU systems. 

### Safety

- CordGuard The convenient metal clip fixes the AC cord tightly and avoids accidental system crashes and data loss. 
- SafeGuard Multiple protection circuitry of OCP (5V/3.3V), OVP, OPP, SCP and SIP. 

### Compatibility

- Intel® Skylake™ Ready! Full support of the C6/C7 energy-saving functions of Intel® processors, first introduced with the Haswell™ generation. 
- Intel ATX 12V v2.4 Compliant with the Intel Power Supply Design Guide for Desktop Platform Form Factors Revision 1.31 April 2013.
- Worldwide Compatibility Automatic 100-240V AC input for global usage and up to 99% active Power Factor Correction (PFC). 

### Quality

- 24/7 @ 40°C Non-stop industrial class performance at 40°C/104°F ambient.
- High-quality Japanese 105°C Electrolytic Capacitors Highest component standards for maximum stability and durability. 
- Compact, Scratch-Resistant Housing Dimensions: 150 mm x 86 mm x 160 mm (W x H x D).
- Warranty 5 Years Manufacturer Warranty.

### Advice

The safety current is 6A per single DC wire. 

Please be noted that overloading could cause the wire or connector to meltdown and void the warranty. You can avoid this by distributing the load to various cables/connectors. 

If you have any questions or doubts how to connect your power supply properly, please do not hesitate to get in contact with our technical support.
