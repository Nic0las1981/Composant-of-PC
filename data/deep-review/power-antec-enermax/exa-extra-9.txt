## NE650C

### NeoECO CLASSIC

POWER SUPPLY

Get economical power today with the NeoECO Classic Series, Antec's high-efficiency power supply solution.

Featuring 80 PLUS® BRONZE certification, a quiet double ball bearing 120mm fan, and universal input with active power factor correction (PFC), the NeoECO Classic series gives you a cooler, quieter system all powered by Continuous Power. The NeoECO Classic series also features industrial safety protection, including short circuit and over voltage protection, for stable and reliable power no matter what.
