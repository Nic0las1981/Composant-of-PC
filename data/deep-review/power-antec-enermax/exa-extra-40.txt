### Product Introduction

The Revolution DUO series is built for cases with tunnel design. Air intakes at the back side of the PSU generate extra air stream in the tunnel. In comparison to single fan PSUs the dual fan design diverts the airflow actively and provides a higher cooling performance. The intelligent fan control optimizes both fans RPM to guarantee a silent operation even at 100% load. The FMA control allows to manually boost the cooling performance of the PSU and to dissipate heat from system components.

### DUO Flow™ Design

 

The DUO Flow™ design greatly improves the airflow inside the PSU. In comparison to single fan PSUs the dual fan design diverts the airflow actively and provides a higher cooling performance. The intelligent fan control optimizes both fans RPM to guarantee a silent operation even at 100% load.

### FMA (Fan-speed Manual Adjustment)

 

The Patented FMA (Fan-speed Manual Adjustment) technology allows to manually boost the cooling performance of the PSU and to dissipate heat from system components. 

 The user can set the FMA borderless to adjust the fan RPM range:

| Auto-Low: | The default setting offers excellent cooling and silent operation for normal use. |
| --- | --- |
| Mid-Rage: | The seamless RPM controller allows a balancing between extra cooling performance and ultra silent operation. |
| High: | The maximum FMA setting generates a strong air flow for extreme cooling conditions. |

### 2nd Generation Twister Bearing Technologie

 

Patented Twister Bearing Technology The bearing shaft is fixed to the rotor. When you turn the fan on, a small magnet inside the bearing holds the shaft in position. The convex surface of the base reduces the frictional resistance and ensures a quiet and smooth fan rotation. The sleeve is made from a self-lubricating material and prevents the bearing from draining and corrosion. That way, Enermax guarantees an exceptionally long lifetime of 160,000 hours (MTBF). Another useful feature of the Twister fans is the “Click Mechanism”. By pressing the rear side of the blades, you can detach the rotor and clean it from dust. Put it back on the base and press it down until you hear a “click”. >> Watch a video about the Twister Bearing Technology
