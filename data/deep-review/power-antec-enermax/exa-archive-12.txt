The Platimax D.F. power supply series is 80 PLUS® Platinum certified and comes in a compact 160mm depth casing, featuring SLEEMAXTM, full modular and individually sleeved cables. Platimax D.F. has a patented build in DFR™ (Dust Free Rotation) technology, which actives at start up to prevent dust accumulation on the fan blades. With the D.F. Switch users can activate the DFR™ function manually at any time. The smart semi-fanless design keeps the fan from running at lower system loads ensuring silent operation.
