# Enermax - REVOBRON

Enermax - REVOBRON

 

 

 

 

 

REVOBRON is the successor of the well-known modular long-life series Triathlor. Durable quality components and a sophisticated technical design ensure stable and clean output voltages. REVOBRON series has the international energy-saving certificate 80 PLUS® Bronze and comes with two independent +12V rails to safely empower systems with high power consumption in 24/7 operation. The semi-modular cable management with flexible and flat cables offers the best options for system installation or expansion.

## Efficiency 

The 80PLUS® Bronze-certified power supply achieves 84 to 91% efficiency at 230VAC between 20 and 100% load. This not only results in low heat generation but also reduces its own CO2 footprint.

REVOBRON helps systems to meet the EU eco-design directive 2013 ErP Lot 6 and 2014 ErP Lot 3 (< 0.5W in standby mode*) due to a high-efficient 5V standby (+5Vsb) circuitry. Moreover, it supports EU regulation no. 617/2013 demanding amongst others 85% efficiency at 50% load and 82% at 20% and 100% load.

* only in combination with an ErP ready mainboard

 

## Power 

The DC-to-DC design generates the secondary voltages (+5V and +3.3V) from the primary voltage rail (+12V). The positive effect is higher efficiency, clean output voltages (low ripple noise) and perfect voltage regulation at all loads.

99 % of the PSU power can be delivered by a multi-rail design with two stable 12V rails to safely empower high-performance processors and graphics cards.

 

## Connectivity 

Modular & Flat Cables simplify the cable routing and improve the airflow inside the case.

Extended 70cm CPU connector cable for installation in large cases.

 

## Cooling 

The integrated 12cm Enermax fan with patented Twister Bearing™ technology ensures an efficient and continuously silent cooling and long lifetime with up to 160,000 hours MTBF.

The patented technology DFR™ (Dust Free Rotation) reduces the dust accumulation on the fan blades. After starting the PSU, the fan spins in reverse at full speed for a few seconds removing dust. Afterwards, it returns to normal operation.

 

## Compatibility 

Intel® ATX12V v2.4, compliant with the Intel Power Supply Design Guide for Desktop Platform Form Factors Revision 1.31 April 2013.

Full support of the C6/C7 energy-saving functions of Intel® processors, first introduced with the Haswell™ generation.

Worldwide compatibility with automatic 100-240VAC input and up to 0.99 with active Power Factor Correction (PFC).

 

## Quality & Safety 

3 Years Manufacturer Warranty.

Enermax only uses high-quality components including first-class Japanese 105°C electrolytic capacitors for maximum stability and durability.

Enermax SafeGuard, the multiple protection circuit that keeps the power supply and the system safe from damage by short circuit (SCP), over voltage (OVP), under voltage (UVP), over temperature (OTP), over current (OCP), over power (OPP) as well as surges and inrush current (SIP).

Non-Stop 24/7 industrial class performance. Full rated power output at 40°C ambient temperature.

No video
