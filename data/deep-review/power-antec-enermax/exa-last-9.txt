### Frequently Asked Questions

Can't find the answer to your question in the manual? You may find the information you need in the FAQs about the Antec TruePower Quattro 850 listed below.

#### Does the Antec TruePower Quattro 850 have 8 SATA power connectors?

Yes, having 8 SATA power connectors means the user can easily power multiple SATA devices such as hard drives and SSDs without the need for additional adapters.

#### Is the Antec TruePower Quattro 850 equipped with a power rocker switch?

Yes, the power rocker switch allows the user to easily turn the power supply on or off, providing convenience and control over the power consumption.

#### What is the total power output of the Antec TruePower Quattro 850 in Watts?

The total power output of the Antec TruePower Quattro 850 is 850 Watts, which is considered high and suitable for powering high-performance PCs or gaming rigs.

#### Does the Antec TruePower Quattro 850 come with an ATX power connector (24-pin)?

Yes, having an ATX power connector (24-pin) ensures compatibility with most modern motherboards, making it easy to install the power supply in a variety of PC builds.

#### What is the Mean Time Between Failures (MTBF) of the Antec TruePower Quattro 850 in hours?

The Mean Time Between Failures (MTBF) of the Antec TruePower Quattro 850 is 100,000 hours, indicating a reliable and durable power supply that is likely to last a long time before encountering any issues.

#### How can I connect the TruePower Quattro 850 PSU to my motherboard?

To connect the TruePower Quattro 850 PSU to your motherboard, use the 24-pin Main Power Connector. If your motherboard uses a 20-pin connector, detach the 4-pin attachment from the 24-pin connector. Additionally, connect either the 8-pin or 4-pin connector for the CPUs as required.

#### The PSU fan is making noise, what should I do?

The TruePower Quattro 850 PSU features a Quiet Computing fan which is designed to run quietly. If the fan noise is unusually loud, ensure the PSU is not overheating. Check that ventilation is adequate and that the PSU fan is not obstructed by dust or debris. Regularly cleaning the fan area can help maintain quiet operation.

#### What should I check if my power supply isn’t turning on?

First, ensure that all power connections are secure. Check that the Main Power Connector and CPU connector are properly attached. Ensure the AC power cord is connected to the power supply inlet and that the power switch is in the 'I' position. Also verify that your outlet is providing power.
