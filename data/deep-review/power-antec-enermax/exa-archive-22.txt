### Power Supply Series

The MaxTytan power supply series is 80 PLUS® Titanium certified and comes in a compact 200mm depth casing, featuring SLEEMAX™, full modular and individually sleeved cables. MaxTytan has a patented build-in DFR™ (Dust Free Rotation) technology, which actives at start up to prevent dust accumulation on the fan blades. With the D.F. Switch users can activate the DFR™ function manually at any time. The smart semi-fanless design keeps the fan from running at lower and mid-range system loads ensuring silent operation.
