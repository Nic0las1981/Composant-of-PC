## Features

80PLUS® efficiency Up to 86% efficiency at 230V and 20-100% load. 80PLUS® certified.

Intel ATX12V v2.3 
Compliant with the latest desktop power supply design guide. Full support of most current CPU: Intel® Core 2 Duo™/ Quad™/ 
Extreme™ / i7™ / i5™ / i3™ and AMD® Athlon™ 64X2/X4 or Phenom™ X3/X4. 
Future ready and flexible 
All-round modular cable management. 10/12P sockets for possible connector changes of upcoming high-performance CPU and 
graphics card generations. 

New: DXXI ready! Full support of most current DX11 graphics cards due to 6+2P (8P) connectors.

Full graphics power Supports SLI™ systems (LibertyECO II 500W/620W/720W) and CrossFireX™ systems.

SpeedGuard Patented fuzzy logic 12cm fan speed control for optimal cooling and minimum noise.

AirGuard Patented air-intake with optimal aero-dynamical design reduces noisy air turbulences.

New: HeatGuard Keeping PSU fan running for 30-60 seconds after shut down to dissipate the remaining system heat and prolonging system lifetime.

SafeGuard Industry-leading octuple protection circuitry of OCP, OVP, AC UVP, DC UVP, OPP, OTP, SCP & SIP.

New: CordGuard Fixing the AC cord tightly to avoid accidental shutdowns of your PC.

Non-Stop @ 40°C Non-stop industrial class performance at 40°C/104°F ambient.

Japanese Main Capacitor Highest component standard for maximum durability and stability.

Worldwide compatibility 100-240V AC input with automatic adjustment and excellent active Power Factor Correction (PFC) for global usage.

Dimensions (B x H x T) 150 x 86 x 140mm

Warranty 3 years manufacturer warranty
