## WD Blue SN580: M.2 PCIe 4.0 NVMe 1.4b SSD

Western Digital's Blue SN580 250GB SSD gives your computer the power of a PCIe 4.0 M.2 SSD at an affordable price. The WD Blue SN580 features a PCIe 4.0 x4 NVMe 1.4b interface, 150TB endurance, 3D NAND memory chips TLC. The WD Blue SN580 250GB (WDS250G3B0) M.2 PCIe 4.0 4x NVMe SSD can propel your PC to speeds of up to 4,000 MB/s read and 2,000 MB/s write, and the number of operations per second reaches 240,000 IOPS read and 470,000 IOPS write (4 KB).

## PCIe 4.0 at the heart of your PC

Whether you're working, creating, playing video games or processing large volumes of data, take advantage of the speed of this powerful internal drive to do more, faster. Benefit from dizzying sequential read speeds of up to 4150 MB/s (depending on the model) to improve your productivity, whatever your activity.
