Get an enhanced PC experience with the all-new KIOXIA EXCERIA PLUS G4 SSD series featuring a state-of-the-art PCIe® 5.0 interface. Offering improved
performance and up to 2TB of capacity for gamers and video editors, the EXCERIA PLUS G4 series delivers low power consumption and reduced heat generation.




Speed Only PCIe® 5.0 Can Provide
Featuring the latest and greatest PCIe® 5.0 interface, the EXCERIA PLUS G4 SSD series delivers excellent transfer speeds
of up to 10,000MB/s for sequential reads and 8,200MB/s for sequential writes. Whether you are loading the latest game
or editing the next viral video, this new series will provide the speed you need.




                                    Power Efficiency Prioritized
                                    The EXCERIA PLUS G4 SSD series delivers significantly improved performance without excessive energy or cooling
                                    requirements. Designed with efficiency in mind, EXCERIA PLUS G4 SSDs offer low power consumption, reducing the heat
                                    generated in your system. These SSDs delivers up to approximately 80% more power efficiency at max sequential read
                                    speed over the previous generation EXCERIA PLUS G3 series.*
                                    * These values are the read out speed per power consumption obtained in a specific test environment at Kioxia Corporation. Power efficiency is the useful output
                                      which is measured in tasks per watt.




     Advanced Heat Dissipation
     EXCERIA PLUS G4 SSDs feature innovative product labels to enhance heat dissipation for advanced performance
     stability.




                                    Cutting Edge 3D Flash Memory
                                    Each EXCERIA SSD is built with BiCS FLASH™ and a vertically stacked cell structure, delivering a cutting edge storage
                                    experience.




     SSD Utility Management Software

     maintenance, monitoring, and more!
     We highly recommend you install and update to the latest version to maximize your drive’s performance and check its
     Percentage Life Left using the health gauge.
