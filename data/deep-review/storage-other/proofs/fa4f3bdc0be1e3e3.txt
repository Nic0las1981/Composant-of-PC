Limited Warranty Condition
The limited warranty period for Solid State Storage ends when either of the
following occurs:
(a) the time limit specified by the warranty expires, or (b) the Solid-State Storage
reaches its endurance limit (TBW – Total Bytes Written). TBW is a measure of the
SSD's expected lifespan. The SSD SMART percentage used value, as indicated by
CrystalDiskInfo or addlink's toolbox, reflects the TBW status.

Reference tools:
• CrystalDiskInfo: https://crystalmark.info/en/software/crystaldiskinfo/
• addlink SSD Toolbox: https://www.addlink.com.tw/ssdtoolbox

Checking SSD Remaining Lifespan
Using either CrystalDiskInfo or the addlink Toolbox, locate the Percentage Used
value (SMART attribute ID 05):
• Percentage Used = 0: New, unused product — full TBW lifespan remaining.
• Percentage Used ≥ 100: Drive has reached or exceeded its TBW endurance
limit. Warranty coverage for endurance-related failures ends at this point.
Duration of Warranty
